LEGO Dimensions Toy Pad

Toy pad

Tap a spot to choose where the next token goes, or just tap a character below to use the next free spot.

Tokens

Only the Flipper's own character and vehicle list can be placed. Characters have no memory to keep; vehicles do (see Saved vehicles below). Favorites stay in this browser.

Saved vehicles

A vehicle keeps its upgrades in its own memory. While a vehicle is on the pad this page asks the Flipper for that memory and keeps a copy here (in this browser's own database, not uploaded). Tap a saved vehicle to put it back exactly as it was. Clearing site data erases the copies, so tap Export now and then.
